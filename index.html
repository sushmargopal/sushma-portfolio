// 01 — about

A trainer who codes, and a coder who loves to teach.

I'm Sushma, a Senior Technical Trainer at Rooman Technologies in Bengaluru. Over the last eight years, I have grown from delivering sessions to designing complete curricula built around practical, project-first learning that gets people hired.

My strength is simplifying complex technical concepts and tailoring them to whoever is in the room, whether that is a fresh graduate writing a first class or an experienced engineer moving to the cloud. I run live coding sessions, build end-to-end projects, mentor trainers and prepare aspirants for interviews.
